Based on the indexed papers, here are the model architectures used for protein structure prediction and their reported accuracy:

## **AlphaFold 2**
Uses advanced neural network architectures that leverage large evolutionary databases and multiple sequence alignments (MSAs). It achieved unprecedented accuracy, effectively solving core aspects of the long-standing protein-folding problem [2508_18446v1__c0001].

## **AlphaFold 3**
Deploys a hierarchical **multi-scale transformer architecture** organized into three parallel modules [2508_18446v1__c0006]:
- **Local-scale transformers**: Focus on short-range interactions among neighboring residues, learning secondary structure elements and local backbone geometry
- **Mid-scale transformers**: Operate at the domain/subdomain level, integrating local features into coherent domain-wise representations and modeling intra-domain contacts and topology
- **Global-scale transformer**: Processes interactions between domains or subunits in complexes, assembling the full tertiary/quaternary structure

Additional architectural innovations include:
- **Biologically-informed cross-attention mechanisms** that integrate evolutionary profiles from MSAs, structural templates, functional motifs, and physico-chemical constraints [2508_18446v1__c0009]
- **Geometry-aware loss functions** optimizing torsion angles, backbone planarity, and stereochemical restraints [2508_18446v1__c0012]

### Reported Accuracy (Figure 3 benchmarks):
| Method | Average GDT_TS | Average TM-score |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| AlphaFold 3 | **95.6%** | **0.97** |
| AlphaFold 2 | 92.4% | 0.92 |

For varying protein lengths (Figure 5), AlphaFold 3 maintains higher accuracy than AlphaFold 2 on longer proteins: short targets (<200 aa) 94.8% vs 93.5%; long targets (>500 aa) 91.2% vs 82.5% [2508_18446v1__c0040].
