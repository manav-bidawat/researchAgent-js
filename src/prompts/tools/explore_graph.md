Explore the knowledge graph neighbourhood around a specific paper, chunk, topic, or figure.
Use this when you need to understand the relationships between entities — for example, to find all chunks belonging to a paper, all papers tagged with a topic, or the chunks immediately preceding and following a specific chunk.
Requires a known `entity_id` (a paper_id, chunk_id, figure_id, or topic name).
Returns the graph paths up to `depth` steps away.
