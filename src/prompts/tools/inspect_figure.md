Look at the actual image of a figure or table from a paper, rather than its stored text
description. Use this when `retrieve_evidence` returned a figure or table and you need
detail the description may have lost — exact values, axis labels, the shape of a trend,
or what a specific series does. Also use it to inspect an image the user has supplied.
Requires a `figure_id` from a previous `retrieve_evidence` result, or a user-supplied
image reference.
